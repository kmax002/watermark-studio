What happens to the video?

SDR video keeps its proportions, up to 1920 pixels on its longest edge, and is encoded as H.264. Variable frame rates are made constant. The first audio track is converted to AAC. Your uploaded original is preserved.

HDR, rotation metadata and non-square pixels need a normalised SDR export first. Seven windows are checked in longer videos; short videos receive one window check. Recovery must agree across both halves of every window.

Storage & privacy

Download your videos and private recovery file, then you can terminate this Pod. To check a leak later, start a fresh Pod and upload only the suspected video and that recovery file. You do not need to keep videos on RunPod. Back up the recovery file somewhere private: it cannot be replaced if lost. A shared template contains the app, never another creator’s keys.
